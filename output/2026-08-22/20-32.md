The U.S. Treasury Department’s buyback announcement this past week wasn’t enough to calm jittery stock and bond markets. But as bond yields climbed and the U.S. dollar weakened, prices of bitcoin and gold shot higher.
Bitcoin , in particular, emerged as a big winner after Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent unveiled plans to intervene in the bond market with larger buybacks of longer-dated Treasurys.
The Treasury Department’s buyback announcement on Aug. 19 was the “main catalyst” for bitcoin’s surge, said Stephen Coltman, head of macro at 21shares, in an interview Friday.
