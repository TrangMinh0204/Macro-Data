A worker produces semiconductors at a production line of a semiconductor manufacturer in Binzhou, China, on Jan. 20, 2025.

Chip stocks have been selling off like mad in the last few weeks, though multiple investors have characterized it as a temporary – although brutal – speedbump on a longer upward trajectory. The PHLX Semiconductor index was up more than 7% as of 2:45PM ET on Thursday.
JPMorgan said the deleveraging has happened more quickly than they expected.
“We find that investors deleveraging in the tech and semiconductor space, including memory stocks, has advanced faster than we had previously anticipated,” Nikolaos Panigirtzoglou at JPMorgan wrote in a Wednesday note to clients. “As a result, we now see more limited room for any further deleveraging.
